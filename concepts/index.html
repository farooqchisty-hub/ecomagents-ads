Video ad concepts: Ecom Agent OS + Winning Statics bundle

Stage 1 of the cinematic harness, 28 Oct 2026. Pick the ones to storyboard. Nothing is generated until you approve a storyboard.

Rules baked into every concept
  • 60 s, 9:16, with a 30 s cutdown. Built through cinematic-ads-harness: dialogue generated natively in the video (Seedance 2.5), about 30 s single-shot units, one take each, brand/price/CTA lines in a short separate unit.
  • Professor register: clear, complete sentences that explain the why, max 2 words a second. The hook names the audience (D2C founders, performance marketers, ecommerce agencies).
  • North American cast and world. Prices said and shown in rupees but isolated in swappable units, so USD cuts need no regeneration.
  • Real UI (library, Brand Brain, dashboard, ChatGPT captures) as overlays while the native voice continues. No dead air over 1 s, sparse quiet SFX, one continuous score, VO-forward mix.
  • No Shopify, no tool names, no testimonials or results claims; any AI person using the product carries "Dramatization · AI presenter". Say "Ee-kom Agents" and "WIN-ing STAT-iks".
  • Cost per finished 60 s ad: about $18 to $22 (about 65 s of Seedance plus keyframes and score). Storyboard stage: about $2 to $4 per concept for stills.

E1Workplace drama60 s

Sixteen Open Roles

A founder's hiring board has sixteen growth roles she can't afford to fill. One folder fills every one.

First 3 s: A wall of sixteen job postings fluttering in an office draft. Founder, to camera: "If you run a D2C brand, you probably need sixteen people you can't afford to hire."
Cast, locations
Dana, about 34, founder of a small home goods brand; her loft office with a cork wall of postings (Meta buyer, CRO lead, retention lead, analyst...).
Product proof
She walks the wall. At each posting a real agent package (Acquire 8, Convert 3, Retain 3, Operate 2) slides over it as a PiP card and the posting flips to "Covered". Itemized value ₹75,889 vs ₹699 once.
Beats
  1. Hook: the wall
  2. Setup: what each role costs her in time
  3. Turn: she opens the folder
  4. Build: postings flip, four groups at a time
  5. Payoff: the wall reads "16 / 16"
  6. CTA unit: price, one-time, link
Device
One world, one continuous walk (two 30 s takes) so her voice never cuts; UI cards over the wall carry the product.
Evidence
Site: "16 specialist Agents", the Acquire/Convert/Retain/Operate split, itemized value.
Risk
Must not imply agents replace employees or guarantee output; line: "the procedures those roles follow, packaged for your AI workspace."

Two marketers, same product, same deadline. One starts from a blank page; one starts from the library.

First 3 s: Split screen, two identical desks at 11pm. Left: a blank canvas. Right: a grid of templates. Text hook: "The largest library of winning ads on the internet."
Cast, locations
Two performance marketers, about 30, in matching apartments (left world colder light, right warmer).
Product proof
Right side picks a template, copies its JSON prompt, pastes into ChatGPT with the product photo (real captures), gets an ad; repeats with new angles. Left side has four rough ideas by midnight. Ends on the site's own "4 vs 40" framing.
Beats
  1. Hook: split desks
  2. Setup: why creative supply decides testing
  3. Turn: one JSON prompt
  4. Build: the right side's grid fills
  5. Payoff: 4 vs 40 directions
  6. CTA unit: four bonuses, price
Device
Split screen is earned: two parallel stories that answer each other. Each world is its own take; right world's voice on top.
Evidence
Site: "Starting from scratch: 4 ... Winning Statics: 40 creative directions".
Risk
Two parallel takes cost a bit more (about $24). Left side must not mock any tool by name.
