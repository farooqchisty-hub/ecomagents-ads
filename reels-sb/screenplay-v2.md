# Organic reel ads, screenplay v2 (29 Oct 2026)

Rewritten after Farooq's notes. Hard rules for this version:
- Presenters: Mia (EA-1), a pretty woman of about 26, and Ryan (WS-1), a handsome man of about 30. Organic creator setting with a podcast mic, like Reel A. The voice stays calm and explanatory (professor register); the casting is young and attractive.
- Ecom Agent OS language: "agent packages", "skills", "workflows", "Brand Brain". Never "files". No folder walkthroughs.
- EA core message: run your whole brand with 16 agents inside ChatGPT or Claude. Shown, not told: a ChatGPT UI replica running the ad creative agent (paste a product link, get an ad; the ad is a real Winning Statics library example), and the retention agent building an email lifecycle in an unbranded email platform (never named).
- WS: the landing page's dashboard tour video. Steal ads that ran 60 to 90+ days, in your category, paste your product link.
- Hooks are built to stop the scroll: big feed-native type with emoji, not locked to the product's brand styling. Spoken hook lines stay calm.
- Prices only on the end card, as a swappable layer. No dashes.

Shared demo assets (real Winning Statics library examples, from the landing page's "Picture your product in these ads" pairs):
- EA ad creative demo: Pawfull salmon skin dog treats. Product link `pawfull.co/products/salmon-skin-treats`. Output ad: the real Pawfull library example ("One ingredient. Nothing else.").
- EA retention demo: the same Pawfull brand, so both demos feel like one store.
- WS demos: a real product photo and library ad pair with no price inside the picture (Cindera hot sauce or Vanta toothbrush, whichever pair is cleaner).
